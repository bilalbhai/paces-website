Accessibility

A website with
access in mind.

We want people to find information, explore their options and contact P.A.C.E.S. in a way that works for them.

Draft accessibility statement — review and user testing pending

Our design target

This website targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA. That is a design and testing goal, not a certification or a claim that every criterion has been met. A full accessibility audit and testing with people who use assistive technology have not been completed.

Features included

  • Semantic page regions, descriptive headings and a skip link.
  • Visible keyboard focus, keyboard-operable navigation and native FAQ disclosure controls.
  • Readable text, responsive layouts and meaningful alternative text for images.
  • Labelled form fields with text errors linked to the relevant input.
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences, with no autoplay carousel.
  • Touch scrolling and keyboard controls for published worker profiles.
  • Contact options that remain easy to reach on small screens.

Using the site

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links and controls. Press Enter or Space to use buttons and FAQ headings. On a worker carousel, focus the scrolling area and use the left or right arrow keys, Home or End. Escape closes the mobile navigation and returns focus to its button.

You can zoom using your browser. Content is designed to reflow on small screens. External form providers, WhatsApp and other linked websites have their own interfaces and accessibility characteristics.

If something is difficult to use

Please call 0401 339 966 to tell the team what you need or ask for information in a different format. You can also use the enquiry form once submissions are active.

Known review items

Screen reader testing, testing by people with disability, external form delivery and final policy approval remain required before a public launch. Photographic content, worker information and external contact settings are also subject to business approval.
