# PACES brand design system

Updated 26 October 2026 from the supplied client Brand Style Summary and final logo.

| Brand colour | Hex | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slate blue | #5B748C | Large headings, navigation, dark buttons |
| Sage | #8FAF8C | Secondary surfaces and decorative accents |
| Cream | #FAF3DD | Warm page backgrounds |
| Coral | #D67A6C | Primary action surfaces and accents |

Poppins 700 for main headings, 600 for subheadings and 400 for body copy. Google Fonts loads online, with system-font fallbacks offline.

The supplied transparent PNG is copied unchanged to `images/logo/paces-logo.png`; the supplied wordmark JPG is `images/logo/paces-wordmark.jpg`. The stacked logo is never stretched or recoloured. The original website logo remains archived as historical audit evidence, not the active brand.

All colours are centralised in `css/style.css`. Accessible supporting shades include #263746 body text, #40576D dark blue, #9B453B small coral text and #14232D text on coral buttons. White on brand blue is approximately 4.86:1; the original coral and sage are used as surfaces rather than small text on white. Large headings may use the original brand colours where contrast permits. This is a design target, not a claim of complete WCAG certification.

Native staff disclosures support tap/click, keyboard and desktop hover. Animations respect reduced motion. No autoplay. Staff placeholder illustrations and service illustrations use the new palette and do not represent actual employees or premises.
