How it works

A plan built together.
Room to grow.

Our five-step approach puts your needs at the centre, from the first conversation to the next review.

  1. Personalised Assessment

    We begin by understanding your abilities, needs and goals. This helps us discuss a support plan that reflects your individual circumstances and the things you want to work towards.

  2. Collaborative Planning

    Participants, families and our team work together to shape the plan. Your preferences and voice help guide the decisions about your support.

  3. Flexible Scheduling

    We discuss flexible timing around your lifestyle and needs. The team will confirm the arrangements and current availability with you.

  4. Customised Programs

    We create activities and programs around your interests and goals. The aim is meaningful support that recognises your strengths and opportunities for growth.

  5. Continuous Review and Adjustment

    We regularly review the plan and adjust it as your needs change. Your feedback is part of keeping the support relevant to your life.
