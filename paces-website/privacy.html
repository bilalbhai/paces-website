Clear information about this website’s enquiry fields and external connections.

Draft website notice — business and privacy review required before launch

About this notice

This notice describes the website as built. It is not a complete approved PACES privacy policy or a claim of legal compliance. Business information about privacy contacts, retention, access requests, overseas disclosures and complaint handling must be reviewed and confirmed before online enquiries are activated.

What an enquiry asks for

The forms ask for your name, phone number, email, relationship to the person seeking support, interested service, preferred contact method, optional suburb, optional worker preference and a message. A referral enquiry also asks whether you are authorised to make the enquiry.

Please share only what is needed to start a conversation. Do not enter diagnoses, NDIS plan numbers, medical records, identity documents or financial details. There is no file upload.

When information is sent

Online submissions are inactive until an authorised external form endpoint is configured. In the current draft, checking a form validates the fields in your browser and does not send the enquiry.

Once activated, submitting an enquiry sends the form information to the configured external form provider, for delivery to an authorised PACES inbox. The intended provider is Formspree. The business must review that provider’s handling, hosting, spam filtering and retention settings before activation. See Formspree’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

Other external connections

The website stylesheet requests fonts from Google Fonts when you have an internet connection. Google receives connection information such as your IP address through that request. System fonts are used if the fonts cannot load. The fonts can be removed or self-hosted by the site owner.

Call links open your device’s calling application. WhatsApp links open the external WhatsApp service using the business’s configured contact number. Facebook and Instagram links open the business’s external social media pages. Information you choose to send there is handled by that service. External links may have their own privacy practices.

Browser storage and analytics

This website’s own code does not set cookies, save enquiry drafts in local storage or include analytics, advertising trackers or embedded maps. A hosting platform or an activated third-party form service may process technical connection data under its own policies.

Questions about your information

Call 02 8488 3731 to ask PACES about handling your information or the correct privacy contact. A dedicated privacy email and approved request procedure have not yet been provided.
