How do I find the right NDIS support provider for myself or a family member?

The right provider should understand your needs, respect your preferences and offer consistent, reliable support. At PACES, we take time to get to know each participant and their goals, matching them with suitable support workers and working closely with families and Support Coordinators to provide personalised support.

Can I choose my NDIS support worker?

Yes. Your comfort, preferences and compatibility matter. At PACES, we aim to match participants with a suitable support worker who understands their personality, interests and support needs, helping build trust and positive relationships.

What NDIS funding can I use for community participation and daily living support?

Depending on your NDIS plan, Core Supports funding may cover assistance with daily living, community participation and social activities. We can discuss which of our services align with your goals and available funding. Suitability and funding arrangements must be confirmed for your individual circumstances.

Can I change my NDIS support provider if I’m unhappy with my current support?

NDIS participants can choose and change support providers, subject to any existing service agreement and notice periods. We understand the importance of finding the right support and can discuss how to help make a transition as smooth and comfortable as possible.

How do I get started with an NDIS provider, and do I need a service agreement?

We begin with a conversation to understand your needs, preferences and goals. Together, we develop a personalised support plan, match you with a suitable team and establish a service agreement outlining the supports, costs and responsibilities before services begin.

What types of disabilities does PACES support?

PACES supports individuals with a range of disabilities, including autism, intellectual disabilities and Down syndrome. We take time to understand each participant’s individual needs and determine whether our team and services are the right fit.

What areas does PACES provide NDIS support services in?

PACES is based in Smithfield, NSW, providing centre-based activities and community support across surrounding suburbs in Western Sydney. Contact our team to discuss service availability in your area.

Does PACES offer group activities and social programs?

Yes. We offer structured group and centre-based activities designed to encourage social connections, develop practical skills and build confidence. Activities are tailored to participants’ interests, abilities and individual goals.

How does PACES work with families and Support Coordinators?

We believe quality support works best when everyone communicates and collaborates. Our team works closely with participants, families, Support Coordinators and allied health professionals to develop personalised supports, share progress and respond to changing needs.

How does PACES help participants build independence and life skills?

Our mentor-driven approach focuses on developing practical skills through everyday experiences. Whether it is community participation, daily routines or learning something new, we encourage participants to build confidence, make choices and work towards greater independence at their own pace.
