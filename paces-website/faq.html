What support does P.A.C.E.S. provide?

Our six services are Community Participation, Home Care, Transportation, Day Programs, Activities and Skill Building, and Weekend Programs. Explore our services to see what each involves.

How is my support personalised?

We start by discussing your abilities, needs and goals. Participants, families and our team plan together, agree on scheduling and activities, and regularly review the support as needs change.

Can my family be involved in planning?

Yes. The support process described by P.A.C.E.S. includes collaborative planning with participants, families and our team, with the participant’s needs and choices guiding the plan.

What can I do in a day or weekend program?

The existing programs include creative arts, outdoor experiences, hobbies and opportunities to relax or learn. Activities are discussed around individual interests and capabilities. Please ask the team about current options.

How do I get started?

Call 0401 339 966 to talk with our team. You can also use the enquiry form once online submissions are activated. An enquiry does not confirm a booking or service availability.

Where does P.A.C.E.S. provide support?

P.A.C.E.S. is a Sydney, NSW disability support business. Please contact our team to check whether we can support you in your suburb and discuss current service availability. No specific suburb coverage is confirmed on this website.

Does transport support include accessibility needs?

The existing transportation service describes wheelchair-accessible vehicles and specialised travel assistance. Please discuss your access requirements with our team so suitability and availability can be confirmed.
