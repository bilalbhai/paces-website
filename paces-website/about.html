About PACES

Two backgrounds.
One shared purpose.

At PACES, understanding the individual comes first. Our approach brings together meaningful relationships, continuous learning and personalised support.

Our story

Prosper and Care Endeavour Services (PACES) began with two different backgrounds and one shared purpose.

With over 20 years of experience in the disability sector, Farah brought knowledge, compassion and an understanding of the challenges faced by participants and their families.

Issa came from an engineering background, seeking a career with greater purpose and the opportunity to make a meaningful difference. His interest in human behaviour and personal development has led him to pursue studies in Social Science, focusing on behavioural studies. This reflects his belief that quality support requires continuous learning, understanding and a willingness to grow.

Together, they established PACES with a simple belief: quality support is about more than providing a service. It is about building relationships, continually learning and working together to help individuals reach their potential.

Today, that belief continues to guide our approach. We focus on quality over quantity, taking time to understand each participant and surrounding them with a team genuinely invested in their progress.

Building bonds to make a difference

We take time to understand each participant’s personality, interests and needs, connecting them with mentor-driven supports that build trust, form meaningful relationships and encourage personal growth.
