About P.A.C.E.S.

Support that sees
the whole person.

We’re Prosper and Care Endeavour Services. Our participants’ needs are our compass, and their possibilities are our purpose.

Welcome to P.A.C.E.S.

Every person brings their own skills, talents and aspirations. At Prosper and Care Endeavour Services, we celebrate those differences and work alongside participants to create support that reflects who they are.

Our approach is guided by the needs of the participant at every step. We aim to provide the tools, resources and encouragement that help people with disability take part in life, pursue their interests and build confidence in personal and professional settings.

From practical skill development to personalised care plans, we identify strengths and areas for growth together. The pathway we create is individual, because everyone’s life and goals are different.

Our mission

To let participants’ needs guide our support, with a commitment to inclusion, empowerment and opportunities to thrive.

Our vision

We want participants to live fulfilling lives with independence, inclusion, growth, well-being and empowerment. That means having the tools, resources and support to live on their own terms, with dignity and purpose.

We envision welcoming, accessible communities where each person feels valued and has a sense of belonging. We support personal and professional development, health and happiness, and opportunities for people to advocate for themselves and others.

Your voice guides the way

We work closely with participants and families to plan support, discuss scheduling and choose meaningful activities. Regular reviews give us an opportunity to listen again and adjust as needs change.
