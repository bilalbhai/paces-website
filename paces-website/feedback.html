Feedback and complaints

Your feedback matters.

You can share feedback or raise a concern with PACES You can also contact the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.

Contact PACES

To share feedback or discuss a concern, call 02 8488 3731. If you would like support to communicate, let the team know the format or assistance that works for you.

An approved internal complaints procedure, response timeframes and a dedicated complaints email were not available in the existing website information. These details are being prepared for business review. We do not publish a procedure or promise a response timeframe that has not been confirmed.

Contact the NDIS Commission

The NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission provides information about raising a complaint about an NDIS provider or worker. You can contact the Commission directly without using this website.

  • Make a complaint to the NDIS Commission (opens in a new tab)
  • Call the NDIS Commission: 1800 035 544

Please use the Commission’s own website for its current process, available assistance and contact options.

Keep sensitive details private

Please call the relevant contact to discuss how to share information securely. Do not include medical records, financial information or detailed allegations about another person in a general website enquiry.
