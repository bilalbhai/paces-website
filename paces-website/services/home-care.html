Home Care

Everyday support, with your dignity, choices and independence at the centre.

Support shaped around you.

Our personalised home care supports people to live more confidently in their own space. We take a holistic approach, looking beyond daily tasks to understand what makes life at home meaningful for you.

The existing P.A.C.E.S. home care offering includes assistance with showering, dressing and mealtime management, as well as household chores and outdoor maintenance. Support is discussed around your individual needs, preferences and the assistance that is appropriate for you.

Home care can also make room for social connection, hobbies and meaningful engagement. Our support workers aim to provide respectful, compassionate assistance while encouraging you to remain involved in the decisions that affect your day.

We discuss occasional assistance or ongoing support with you and adapt the plan as your needs change. Contact the team to talk about the type of support you need and current suitability and availability.

What support may look like

  • Assistance with personal routines such as showering and dressing.
  • Support with meals and mealtime management.
  • Help with household chores and outdoor maintenance.
  • Support to pursue hobbies, social outings and connections with peers.

Working towards your goals

Home care may support goals around everyday independence, dignity and well-being. The focus is on practical help that respects your preferences while creating opportunities for confidence, connection and personal growth.
