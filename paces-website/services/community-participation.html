Community Participation

Community experiences that reflect your interests, choices and goals.

Support shaped around you.

At PACES, community participation is about more than getting out of the house. We take time to understand each participant’s interests, abilities and goals, planning outings that are enjoyable and meaningful.

Whether it is shopping, visiting local attractions, attending social activities or learning to use public transport, our team encourages participants to make choices, build confidence and develop practical skills.

We focus on consistency and building trust, matching participants with mentors who understand their needs and can support them through new experiences and challenges.

Our aim is for every outing to offer something valuable, whether that is a new skill, greater confidence or a positive social experience.

What support may look like

  • Shopping and visiting local attractions.
  • Attending social activities and meeting other people.
  • Learning to use public transport.
  • Developing practical skills through meaningful outings.

Working towards your goals

Your choices guide the experiences we plan together. We aim to make space for connection, confidence and practical learning while respecting your individual needs and pace.
